About

Short version

BYU Marriott finance grad, DevMountain full-stack bootcamp, fluent in Spanish. I’ve owned P&L and FP&A work for Oculus launches at Facebook, cut Project Wing’s US ops footprint at Google X, and built analytics dashboards at Intuit. Outside big tech I founded ScanFactor, run a Rexburg student-housing portfolio, and keep building through land investing.

I also wrote Seeking An Edge — the playbook from my path into Intuit, Google, and Facebook (and interviews or offers from Apple, HPE, GE, and Intel) without being an engineer or Ivy League.
